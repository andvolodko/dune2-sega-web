📖 Commander Tactical Manual

The Spice Must Flow:

Arrakis is the only source of the Spice Melange in the known universe. Three Houses vie for supreme domination: the noble Atreides, the brutal Harkonnen, and the insatiable Ordos.

Tactical Mechanics:

  • Concrete Foundations: Always lay down concrete slabs before placing buildings to prevent 50% initial structural damage.
  • Power Balance: Monitor your Windtraps closely. When power runs low, radar and production times drop drastically.
  • Harvesters & Carryalls: Build a Repair Facility early to unlock Carryall transport flyers that automatically shuttle damaged tanks and full Harvesters.
  • Sandworms: Watch for spice vibrations on the open sand! Sandworms swallow heavy vehicles whole. Keep units on rocky outcrops whenever possible.

Unique House Arsenals:

  • House Atreides: Sonic Tanks (destructive acoustic waves) and Fremen infantry stealth commandos.
  • House Harkonnen: Devastator heavy siege tank (self-destruct capable) and Death Hand tactical nuclear missiles.
  • House Ordos: Deviator gas launcher (temporarily turns enemy tanks to your side) and ultra-fast Raider trikes.
